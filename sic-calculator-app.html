Calculation Formulas

Inputs in mm, converted to cm internally. Output in cm³ / grams.

ID = OD − (2 × Wall Thickness) A = π/4 × (OD² − ID²) [hollow] A = π/4 × OD² [solid] Volume = A × Length [cm³] Weight = Volume × Density [grams] C_mat = (Weight / 1000) × ₹/kg Margin = C_mat × (Margin% / 100) Item_cost = C_mat + Margin [excl. GST] CGST = Item_cost × 9% SGST = Item_cost × 9% Item_GST = Item_cost + CGST + SGST Unit_Price = Item_GST + Shipping_cost Total = Unit_Price × Qty
